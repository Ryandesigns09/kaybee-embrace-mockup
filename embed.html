Founder

Kay Beach

CD (DONA) · CPD · CNPE · CPNE · AdvNCS · SpBCPE · CBE · CLSC · VCD

Kay is a birth and postpartum doula, lactation counselor, newborn care specialist and educator. Her approach comes from her Garifuna heritage and her own early years as a young mom in the Caribbean, surrounded by a community that brought meals, did the laundry and held the baby so she could rest.

When she moved to the United States she saw that not every family has that village. Kaybee Village is her answer: ancestral and cultural wisdom, blended with modern evidence-based care, and a collective of birth workers who show up together.
